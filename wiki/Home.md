# Axecleft's Tool Suite

🌐 **English** · [Français](fr-Accueil) · [Español](es-Inicio) · [Português (BR)](pt-BR-Início) · [Português (PT)](pt-PT-Início)

A set of Foundry VTT modules for the **D35E** (D&D 3.5) system that build characters and NPCs, generate treasure and hoards, run shops, craft items, build traps, put a character sheet on a phone and print paper sheets, all by the 3.5 SRD.

| Module | What it adds |
|---|---|
| **Axecleft's Gemstones** | SRD gems, art objects and jewelry, plus the shared Treasure Generator and Merchant Generator |
| **Axecleft's Armory** | Weapons, armor, shields and ammunition: mundane, masterwork, special materials and magic; the Forge |
| **Axecleft's Curios** | Potions, scrolls, wands, rings, rods, staffs, wondrous items and their psionic equivalents; the Curios Workshop |
| **Axecleft's Traps, Poisons, and Diseases** | SRD traps, poisons and diseases; the Random Trap Generator, the Poison and Disease Creator and trap kits |
| **Axecleft's Hoard Editor** | The Treasure Generator's **Hoards** tab: build, fill, save, place and share out treasure hoards |
| **Axecleft's Adventurers and Minions** | Complete characters and NPCs: build step by step, random (one or many), and edit existing actors |
| **Axecleft's VooDoo** | A player's character sheet on their phone: mobile accounts, a touch window for the sheet, chat, combat and the map |
| **Axecleft's Printable Sheets** | Paper character sheets and stat blocks, and printouts of merchants, loot, hoards, traps and companions |
| **Axecleft's Tool Suite** | Turns the Axecleft modules on and off and checks for their updates, on Foundry 11 and 13–14 |

Each module works on its own, and they work better together. The **Treasure Generator**, **Merchant Generator**, **Crafting** windows and the **Axecleft's Shop** sheet are shared: whichever modules you install add their own tabs and items to them.

## Where to start

- New to the suite? Read [Getting Started](Getting-Started).
- Rolling treasure for an encounter: [Treasure by Level](Treasure-by-Level).
- Setting up shops: [Merchant Generator](Merchant-Generator), then trading on the [shop sheet](Axecleft's-Shop).
- Loot chests and the party's stash: [Loot and party storage](Axecleft's-Shop#loot-containers-and-party-storage).
- Treasure hoards: [Hoard Editor](Hoard-Editor).
- Making characters and NPCs: [Adventurers and Minions](Adventurers-and-Minions).
- Players on phones: [VooDoo](VooDoo).
- Printing sheets: [Printable Sheets](Printable-Sheets).
- Letting players make items: [Crafting](Crafting).
- Traps for a dungeon: [Random Trap Generator](Random-Trap-Generator).

## Versions

- **Foundry VTT 13 and 14** with D35E 3.x: module versions **1.14.x**.
- **Foundry VTT 11** with D35E 2.4.3: module versions **1.11.x** (separate downloads). Intelligent items need D35E 3.1 or later, so they are hidden on Foundry 11.

## Tours

Most windows have guided tours: **Configure Settings → Tour Management**, under the module's name, or **Tour of this tab** in the window's ⋮ menu (Foundry 13–14). On Foundry 11 a **Tour** button sits in the window's title bar.

## Support the suite

Every module is free. If they help at your table, you can support their development on [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/Axecleft): patrons get new releases two weeks before everyone else. The welcome window that opens when Foundry starts has the link, and so does the **Axecleft's Tool Suite** button in each module's settings.

## Getting help

Ask questions, share ideas and follow the news on [Axecleft's Tool Suite Discord server](https://discord.gg/FRhPPwDWta).

Report problems or ideas on the module's GitHub page. Please say which module and version, which Foundry and D35E versions, and what you clicked; a screenshot of the window and of the browser console (F12) helps a lot.
