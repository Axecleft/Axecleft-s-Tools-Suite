# Random character (Adventurers and Minions)

**Where:** [Adventurers and Minions](Adventurers-and-Minions) → **Random character**.

A random character or NPC, or many at once. Choose what matters; anything left on **Random** is chosen for you by the class profiles, modelled on the DMG's sample NPCs.

> 📷 **Screenshot:** the Random character page, with Race, Class, Build, Total level, Prestige class, How many and Generate.

## The choices

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **PC** or **NPC** | A player character (character sheet, PC wealth) or an NPC (NPC sheet, NPC gear value). |
| **Race**, **Sex**, **Alignment** | Or **Random**: the race from the common races, an alignment the class allows. |
| **Class** | A class and how many levels in it, or **Random** (NPC classes often for NPCs). Variant classes (Unearthed Arcana) are in their own group of the list; Random never picks one by itself. |
| **Second class** | A second class and its levels, or Random, makes a multiclass character. Random class mixes share an alignment. |
| **Build** | For a class with several builds (a fighter's archer or two-handed, a wizard's evoker or summoner…): one of them, or **Random**. The build sets the ability order, skills, feats, spells and gear. See [Builds](AM-Variants-and-Builds#builds). |
| **Gestalt** | Two classes at every level (Unearthed Arcana): the class and second class each at the total level, random when not chosen. See [Gestalt](AM-Variants-and-Builds#gestalt). |
| **Total level** | The character's level. Blank: 1 to 5 (or the class levels given). |
| **Prestige class** | **None**, **Random** once the character qualifies, or one prestige class. See below. |
| Ability scores | How the scores are made (arrays, rolled or point buy), placed by the class profile. |
| Hit points | How hit points are rolled. |
| **How many** | More than one goes straight into the folder, without Review: a band of guards, a party of adventurers. |
| **Owner** | GM only: a player who will own the new actors (the GM always does). |
| **Generate** | Makes the character and opens it on Review. |

The other button beside **Generate** makes that many characters at once, straight into the folder. It reads the choices when you press it, and reports any character it couldn't make.

## What A&M chooses

Each class follows its profile (its build): the order its scores go in and where its increases go, the skills it buys (raised together, up to their maximum), the feats it takes as their prerequisites allow (Weapon Focus with its favourite weapon, and so on), its likely alignments, its favourite spells and its gear. Classes without a profile get one worked out from the class. The GM can change the builds: see [Build profiles](AM-Variants-and-Builds#build-profiles).

Random characters get spells and equipment as on the [Spells](AM-Build#spells) and [Equipment](AM-Build#equipment) steps, domains and specialist schools included (wizards specialize half the time).

## Prestige class

With **Random** or a chosen prestige class, the character takes it from the first level it meets the requirements (from 6th for a random one), then the levels after it in that class, up to its maximum. The requirements are the SRD's or your own (see [Prestige requirements](AM-Variants-and-Builds#prestige-requirements)).
- The skills and feats from then on are chosen again; the levels before stay.
- When no prestige class qualifies, the character keeps its classes and a note says so.

## After Generate

The character opens on **Review**:
- **Roll again** any part: name, abilities, hit points, skills, feats, spells, equipment, or everything. Rolling the abilities redoes skills and feats too.
- Or open any step and change it, as on [Build a character](AM-Build).
- Then **Create actor**.
