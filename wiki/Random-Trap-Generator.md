# Random Trap Generator (Traps, Poisons, and Diseases)

**Where:** **Axecleft's Tools → Random Trap Generator**, or the **Random Trap** button at the top of the Actors sidebar. GM only.

Roll random traps by Challenge Rating and create D35E trap actors. Traps are built from SRD sample traps and the SRD trap-design rules, including poison and disease traps.

> **<img width="1307" height="698" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f2220c27-8db2-44b1-b1f8-853753150e43" />**

## The window

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Challenge Rating** | CR 1 to 20. |
| **Trap** | **Random**, or a trap for that CR. SRD sample traps are marked **(SRD)**, poison and disease traps **(poison/disease)**. The list changes with the CR and the two boxes below. |
| **How many** | 1 to 10 traps. |
| **Include SRD sample traps** | Allow the SRD's sample traps (fixed stats). |
| **Include poison & disease traps** | Allow traps built around a poison or disease. |
| **Create actors** | Create a trap actor for each trap. Untick to only post the stat lines to chat. |

Buttons:
- **Generate**: rolls the traps.
- **Customize…**: opens the Customize window for one trap (see below).
- **Cancel**.

## Results

- A chat card, **Random Trap — CR {cr}**, lists each trap's stat line.
- With **Create actors**, each trap becomes a D35E trap actor in the **Random Traps** Actors folder. Its notes hold the trigger, save DC and effects, ending with "Generated by the Random Trap Generator."
- A trap posted without an actor has a **Create Actor** button on the chat card, so you can keep the ones you like.

## Customize

Choose a trap's values yourself. **Customize: {trap} (CR {cr})** opens for the trap you picked, or, on **Random**, a random trap that isn't an SRD sample trap.

> **<img width="618" height="637" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b8728088-d297-4c9b-9a0c-7aa7e993a6a6" />**

### Ordinary traps

Leave anything blank to roll it randomly. The boxes suggest the values the generator uses, but you can type any value.

| Section | Controls |
|---|---|
| **Trap** | **Type**, **Trigger**, **Reset**, **Bypass** (each a list, or Random); **Attack bonus**, **Damage**, **Reflex save**, **Search DC**, **Disable Device DC** (boxes; hover for the suggested values). |
| **Effect** / **Duration** | When the trap has alternatives: **Choose** one, or Random. Then its values. |
| **Poison** | For a poisoned trap: an SRD injury or contact poison (with its DC and CR modifier), or **Random (similar strength)**. |

### Poison and disease traps

Each choice shows its SRD CR modifier. Leave a choice on **Random** and the generator fills it in so the trap comes as close to the chosen CR as it can. The final CR is the sum of the modifiers.

Choices: **Poison / disease**, **Base trap**, **Pit depth**, **Reflex save**, **Targets**, **Spikes**, **Onset delay**, **Attack bonus**, **Damage**, **Search DC**, **Disable Device DC**.

### Buttons

- **Create Trap** (with **Create actors** ticked) creates the actor; **Post to Chat** (with it unticked) posts the stat line only.
- **Cancel**.

SRD sample traps have fixed stats, so they can't be customized: they're generated as they are.
