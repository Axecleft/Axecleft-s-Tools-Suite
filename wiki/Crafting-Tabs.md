## Magic arms and armor

Add an enhancement bonus and special abilities to a masterwork weapon, armor, shield or ammunition (SRD), or upgrade a magic one (below). Magic supplies cost half the magic's base price in gp and 1/25 in XP; the masterwork item is the item cost.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Masterwork or magic item** | Where the base comes from: **Buy it (pay the item cost)**, **From the crafter's inventory**, **From {name}'s inventory** (another character's), or **Make it first with Craft** (raw materials ⅓ of its price; the magic is added when the checks succeed). |
| **Item** | With an inventory source: the masterwork or magic item to enchant. From a stack of arms or armor, one item is used. |
| **Base item** | When buying or making it: the weapon, armor, shield or ammunition. |
| **Material** | A special material, when buying or making it. |
| **Enhancement** | +1 to +5, and **+6 to +20 (epic)** with **Epic** ticked. |
| **Epic** | Allow epic enhancement. Needs **Epic items** on in [Generation Settings](Generation-Settings#campaign). |
| **Ability 1, 2…** | Special abilities. SRD abilities carry their prerequisites in Armory's data. Psionic abilities need Craft Psionic Arms and Armor, with powers in place of spells. |
| **Bane foe** | For *bane*: the creature type. |
| **Sheds light** | **No** or **Yes**. SRD: the creator decides now; it can't be changed later. |
| **Look** | The light's look: **From its abilities (or neutral)**, or a theme. |
| **Intelligent** | Make it intelligent (SRD: caster level 15; it takes the crafter's alignment). Its mind, powers and any special purpose are rolled when the GM approves, and their price (from +1,000 gp) is added, so the gp, XP and time rise. |
| **Pieces** | Ammunition: magic ammunition is made 50 at a time; fewer pieces cost proportionally less. With ammunition from the inventory, it's set to the stack's pieces (50 at most) and can't be changed. |

- **Caster level**: at least 3 × the enhancement bonus, and each ability's own caster level.
- **Custom abilities** (from Shared Data or your own enhancement items) are read from their description's construction line. See [How requirements are found](Crafting#how-requirements-are-found). Without one, the GM decides.
- **Double weapons** count as two weapons for cost, time, XP and abilities (SRD). Enchant each head separately.
- When it's done, the chosen item becomes the magic item.

### Upgrading a magic item

The DMG's **Adding New Abilities** rule (p. 288) lets a crafter add to a magic item. Choose a magic weapon, armor, shield or ammunition from the inventory:

- **Enhancement** is its new bonus. It can't go lower than it is.
- **Abilities** are the ones to add. Its existing abilities stay: the magic already in it is locked in. A *+2 flaming longsword* can become a *+2 flaming flaming burst longsword* or a *+3 flaming frost longsword*, but not a *+3 frost longsword*.
- The cost, XP and time are the **difference** between the new item's price and the old one's.
- It needs **every prerequisite of the new item**, old abilities included, as if it were made from scratch: the crafter works with the magic already in it without harming it. That includes a caster level of at least 3 × the new bonus.
- The DMG's 50% surcharge for items that take a body slot isn't applied to arms and armor: raising a bonus or adding an ability is the same kind of magic.
- Its light was decided when it was made, so **Sheds light** isn't offered.
- Only magic items the Armory made are listed (its forge, generators or crafting), since their magic can be read back. Cursed and intelligent items aren't listed.

## Specific magic arms

Specific magic weapons, armor, shields and ammunition (*Flame Tongue*, *Celestial Armor*…) from your item compendiums.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Kind** | **Weapons**, **Armor and shields**, or **Ammunition**. |
| **Item** | Type to search. |

- The requirements come from the SRD statblock in the item's description: its caster level, spells and other prerequisites.
- Cost: the masterwork item, plus half of the magic's price in gp, and 1/25 in XP. A **Cost** in the statblock replaces it.

## Arms and armor (Craft)

> **<img width="621" height="705" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/74793b01-f562-4fdd-93a2-6d6b27d8cbf3" />**

Mundane weapons, armor, shields and ammunition made with Craft (SRD).

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Item** | The weapon, armor, shield or ammunition. |
| **Masterwork** | Make it masterwork. Adamantine, mithral, darkwood and dragonhide always are. |
| **Material** | A special material. |
| **Pieces** | For ammunition. |

- Raw materials cost ⅓ of the price. The DC comes from the SRD Craft table.
- A masterwork item has a separate masterwork component (DC 20), with its own check.
- A check failed by 5 or more ruins half of that part's raw materials.

## Scrolls, potions, wands

> **<img width="622" height="790" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/523fd2b4-eb26-4824-bf75-e61d830ac239" />**
> **<img width="616" height="486" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7855a84d-a90e-40c2-9435-e1ebc94befb7" />**

Scrolls, potions and wands, and (while Psionics is on) power stones, psionic tattoos and dorjes (SRD). Supplies cost ½ of the base price and the XP 1/25 of it.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Item** | **Scroll** (Scribe Scroll), **Potion or oil** (Brew Potion), **Wand** (Craft Wand), **Power stone** (Imprint Stone), **Psionic tattoo** (Scribe Tattoo), **Dorje** (Craft Dorje). |
| **Spells** / **Powers** | Scrolls and power stones: one per line; several make one master scroll or power stone. Potions, tattoos, wands and dorjes hold one. Add **@** and a caster level: `Fly @7`. |
| **Scroll type** | **As the crafter casts it**, **Arcane** or **Divine**. |
| **Caster level** | Blank: the lowest that can cast it. No higher than the crafter's own. |
| **How many** | Potions and tattoos: each one costs and takes the same (SRD: no savings for brewing several). |

- **Metamagic**: add `+empower`, `+enlarge`, `+extend`, `+heighten N`, `+maximize`, `+quicken`, `+silent`, `+still` or `+widen` after the spell. Each raises its effective level (price and minimum caster level), and the crafter needs the feat.
- **Level limits**: potions stay at 3rd level, wands at 4th and scrolls at 9th, counting the metamagic. The GM's higher limits on the Curios tab don't apply to crafting.
- **Epic**: past 9th level or caster level 20 the item is epic. Scrolls go up to 19th level and caster level 30 (Scribe Epic Scroll). Potions and wands keep their level limit but can go past caster level 20.
- The **spell picker** under the Spells box lists every spell by level, with a caster level box and metamagic checkboxes, and adds the spell to the box. It only offers spells the item can hold, counting the metamagic. See [the spell picker](Curios-Workshop#the-spell-picker).
- Each spell must be provided every day of work. See [Spells](Crafting#spells).

## Rings, rods, staffs, wondrous

>**<img width="621" height="891" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/08f0e25b-2c0b-4485-852f-511a28965d97" />**

Rings, rods, staffs and wondrous items (and psionic universal items) from your item compendiums (SRD). The base price is the market price; supplies cost half of it, and the XP is 1/25.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Kind** | Ring, rod, staff or wondrous item (and psionic universal items while Psionics is on), each with its feat. |
| **Item** | Type to search. Epic items are listed while **Epic items** is on. |
| **Intelligent** | Make it intelligent (SRD: caster level 15; it takes the crafter's alignment). Its mind and powers are rolled when the GM approves, and their price is added. |
| **Sheds light** | **No** or **Yes**. The creator decides now, as the SRD says for arms and armor. Its look follows the item's school of magic. |

- The prerequisites come from the item's own construction line. See [How requirements are found](Crafting#how-requirements-are-found).

## Alchemical items

> **<img width="624" height="688" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c42ce538-9be8-4ef5-8ac4-46f4866aa78c" />**

Craft (alchemy) by the SRD: raw materials cost ⅓ of the price; one check against the item's DC.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Item** | The SRD alchemical items, plus alchemical items from your item compendiums (by name, or tagged `Shop: Alchemical item`). Shown with price and DC. |
| **Craft DC** | For an item that isn't in the SRD: choose its DC from the SRD Craft table. |
| **How many** | Made together: one check, the price of them all for the time. |

- Failing by 4 or less makes no progress; by 5 or more ruins half the raw materials.
- Only a spellcaster can use Craft (alchemy). An alchemist's lab gives +2.

## Crystals, psicrowns

>**<img width="620" height="737" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8173e890-74a9-47ef-ad6f-9020dc5733fb" />**

Only while Psionics is on (Treasure Generator → [Generation Settings](Generation-Settings)). Supplies cost ½ of the price and the XP 1/25 of it.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Item** | **Cognizance crystal** (Craft Cognizance Crystal) or **Psicrown** (Craft Psicrown). |
| **Capacity** | Cognizance crystal: its power points, with the price. Its manifester level is its capacity. |
| **Psicrown** | An SRD psicrown (with its power points, manifester level and price), or **Custom: the powers I list**. |
| **Powers** | Custom psicrown: one power per line. The crafter must know each one. |
| **Manifester level** | Custom psicrown: at least 8th and the highest power's minimum, at most 5 above it. |

- A psicrown holds 50 power points per manifester level (SRD: Craft Psicrown, manifester level 12).
- A custom psicrown's price uses the SRD staff formula (Axecleft house rule).

## Trap kits

> **<img width="614" height="685" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/28b0dcba-3075-4bdf-a6b4-89e4929b8ba5" />**

Craft (trapmaking) by the SRD: mechanical traps only. The result is a **Trap Kit** item that the character can set somewhere. See [Trap Kits](Trap-Kits).

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Trap** | A mechanical trap actor, listed as "CR {cr}: {name}". Grouped by source: **This world** (trap actors in the Actors sidebar you can see, except placed traps) and each compendium set in **Trap kits: compendiums for crafting**. |

- Raw materials cost ⅓ of the trap's price. Poisons in it are bought at full price (×20 with an automatic reset).
- Each week's progress is a check: check × DC in silver pieces (SRD), or the coin set in the **Trap kits: crafting progress** setting (copper, gold or platinum pieces). See [Trap Kits](Trap-Kits#settings).
- The trap needs a price: add "Market Price - 1,000 gp" (its SRD price) to its notes if it has none.
- Magic traps can't be crafted; they come from spellcasting.
- Traps from the [Random Trap Generator](Random-Trap-Generator) are in the GM's world. To let a player craft one, the GM shows it to them (Observer permission).
