# Treasure by Level

Rolls the SRD **Treasure** table for an encounter level: coins, goods (gems and art objects) and items (mundane, minor, medium and major magic items). Each part is made by the module that supplies it; anything without its module is listed in the chat card for you to handle.

> **<img width="602" height="781" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/db5d1109-b2c4-452b-80e9-2856290c0ff0" />**

## Encounter Level

Choose **EL 1** to **EL 30**.
- **EL 1–20**: the SRD table row for that level.
- **EL 21–30** (DMG): the EL 20 row plus 1, 2, 4, 6, 9, 12, 17, 23, 31 or 42 extra major items. The list shows the count, for example "EL 23 (EL 20 + 4 major items)". While **Epic items** is on in [Generation Settings](Generation-Settings), a share of those extra items are epic.

Below the controls, a preview line shows the table row: the d% ranges and dice for coins, goods and items at that level.

## Roll

Tick the parts to roll: **Coins**, **Goods**, **Items**. Untick one to skip it, for example to roll only the coins for a pile you've already stocked.

## Treasure rolls

How many times to roll the table **for each target** (1 to 10). With **Selected tokens**, each token gets this many rolls.

## Sources

A small table shows which module makes each part of the treasure:
> **<img width="605" height="143" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a8764ecb-0843-4999-9bcc-12b0e01bc5f1" />**

| Part | Made by |
|---|---|
| Coins | Built in (paid as set in Coins below) |
| Gems and art objects | Gemstones |
| Alchemical items, tools and gear | Built in, from D35E's items in your item sources |
| Poisons | Poisons in your item compendiums, such as Traps, Poisons, and Diseases (see Poisons in [Generation Settings](Generation-Settings#poisons)) |
| Weapons, armor and shields (masterwork and magic) | Armory |
| Potions, rings, rods, scrolls, staffs, wands, wondrous items | Curios |

A **✔** means that part is covered. **✘ needs [module]; listed in chat** means the module isn't active: that part is rolled and named in the chat card, but no item is made.

## Coins

> **<img width="608" height="391" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/01ccc446-ccc9-4859-99c8-b74e38b95825" />**

The SRD rolls copper, silver, gold and platinum. Here you choose how each is paid:

- **Pay cp / sp / gp / pp as**: as rolled, or converted to another currency. That can be another standard coin or one of D35E's **custom currencies** (set up in D35E's Currency Configuration). Amounts convert by gp value. Anything that doesn't make a whole coin is paid as change in standard coins.
- **Standard coins go**: **In the carried purse** (they have weight), or **In the weightless coins** (D35E's weightless currency).

A line underneath shows an example, such as "1,234 gp + 56 sp + 78 cp is paid as…". These choices are saved for the world.

## Generation settings

> **<img width="609" height="659" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c1eed2dd-0763-49b7-a550-10b639594723" />**

At the bottom of the tab, the **Generation settings** section controls the extras that are added to random items: light, intelligence, inscriptions, sizes, poisons, curses, and the Psionics and Epic campaign switches. See [Generation Settings](Generation-Settings).

## Tips

- To loot a room, select the monsters' tokens, set **Put items in** to **Selected tokens**, and generate. Each creature gets its own pile.
- To see what a level gives before committing, use **Chat card only** and create just the items you want from the card.
- For automatic loot on every NPC by its CR, see [Token Loot](Token-Loot).
